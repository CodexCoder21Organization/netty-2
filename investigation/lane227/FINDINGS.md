OBSERVED source mechanism: NioIoHandler.prepareToDestroy snapshots selector registrations, closes those channels, and callbacks register more channels synchronously. SingleThreadEventExecutor finalization transitions to ST_SHUTDOWN and calls confirmShutdown once, ignoring its result. registerForIo0 does not check terminal admission. Closing newly accepted channels only in that final pass can also reject their delayed deregistration tasks.
INFER / mechanism to fix: IO registration admission must close when shutdown confirmation commits to terminate, before the last channel-close callbacks. One final preparation and task drain must run while graceful shutdown still permits deregistration tasks. This leaves grace-period registrations supported, including the existing shutdown-hook case, while preventing callbacks from extending accepted IO ownership beyond termination.
